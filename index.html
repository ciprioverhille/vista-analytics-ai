01 — Context

Pro-level video analysis. For every coaching staff.

In professional football, every match is broken down by a team of analysts. Elsewhere, staff film their games but lack the time and tools to make use of 90 minutes of play. Ciprio brings that precise, data-driven reading to every club.

02 — Method

Four steps, from final whistle to report.

  1. Step 1

    Video upload

    The full match, filmed from the stand or the touchline. No editing required.

  2. Step 2

    Game plan

    Formation, principles and specific instructions, written freely, the way you would brief the dressing room.

  3. Step 3

    Squad identification

    Your team colour and the name behind every number, substitutes included. When a player or substitution is unclear, the model asks your staff to confirm.

    Number 14 on at 63:00 — Karim?Confirmed
    Number 7 moved to the right at 70:00?Confirmed
  4. Step 4

    Report

    Game plan, team and individual, with every finding linked to its video clips.
