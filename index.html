01 — Context

Pro-level video analysis. For every coaching staff.

In professional football, every match is broken down by a team of analysts. Elsewhere, staff film their games but lack the time and tools to make use of 90 minutes of play. Ciprio brings that precise, data-driven reading to every club.

Three levels of analysis, one report.

What worked

  1. Compact block, 30 m max

    Held 81% of the time. Only 2 shots conceded from central areas.

    Clips · 12:40 · 33:05 · 61:18
  2. High press in the first hour

    11 recoveries in the opposition half, including the one that led to the opening goal.

    Clips · 22:51 · 38:12 · 47:30
  3. Overlaps on the left after 50'

    Moussa (3) delivered 4 crosses. The winning goal came from one of them.

What didn't work

  1. Short build-up from the goalkeeper

    Abandoned after 30'. 18 long clearances, 14 of them lost.

    Clips · 31:10 · 44:26 · 58:03

    5 switches out of 22 opportunities. Play stayed on the right.
