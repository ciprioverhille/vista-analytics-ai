About

Built by a player, for the people on the bench.

Ciprio started with a simple frustration: the tools that help professional clubs read a match are out of reach for almost everyone else.

Ciprio Verhille Founder · Toulouse, France

I'm Ciprio Verhille, I'm 20 and I live in Toulouse. Football has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

I played for more than ten years, at regional and departmental level. Over those seasons I saw how much work coaches put into preparing a match, and how little time and few tools they had afterwards to check what had really happened on the pitch.

At the same time, artificial intelligence is changing how video can be read and understood. I want that change to reach sport, and above all the sport I care about most. Ciprio is my way of making it happen: a tool that compares what a coach asked for with what the team actually did.

I started this project on my own, with the help and support of people close to me. Ciprio is still being built. The site you are reading presents the product I am working towards, and the people who join the waitlist will be the first to try it.

What guides the project

01

The coach's plan comes first

Ciprio does not judge a match against an abstract ideal. It measures it against the instructions the staff actually gave.

02

Honest, checkable analysis

Every finding should be linked to the moments of the match that support it, so the staff can check it for themselves.

03

Built for every level

Amateur, semi-pro and academy staffs deserve the same quality of analysis as professional clubs.
